# Khushi Portfolio — Tactile Redesign

This redesign translates the qualities of Jackie Zhang's portfolio into an original Khushi-specific system rather than copying its artwork.

## New homepage structure

1. PORTFOLIO identity mark with the final O becoming Khushi's face.
2. Personal working belief inside a tactile illustrated portal.
3. A dark product desk where real problems arrive as physical notes.
4. A visible Understand → Structure → Build process.
5. Four selected product stories presented as handmade project sheets.
6. Designer's eye / developer's hands / product mind positioning.
7. Clear email, LinkedIn and GitHub conversion paths.

## Current rollback direction

- Based on the clean animated version before the forced sidebars and pinned-card experiment.
- Removed the orbit around PORTFOLIO and removed repeated portrait usage.
- Added one continuous maker's thread that is drawn by page scroll across actual sections.
- Replaced the repeated central portrait with a tangle-to-product workflow loom.
- Added direct Examon, GitHub and Vercel proof links to the opening and contact areas.

## Product-page polish

- The final PORTFOLIO O now uses the supplied Khushi artwork directly, without a circle or badge container.
- All seven product pages use a new creative case-file template.
- Added problem/solution cards, product blueprint flow, stack chips, ownership and evidence panels.
- Product screenshots now appear as staggered, numbered scrapbook sheets.
- Added stronger live/source actions, takeaway section and next-case transition.

## Query-to-product story revision

- Removed the homepage proof strips and removed the maker's thread completely.
- Cropped and aligned the supplied Khushi artwork so it occupies the final O letter slot.
- Replaced separate problem and product sections with a single pinned Queries → Products sequence.
- Every query reveals its corresponding product; completed cards remain stacked underneath.
- Homepage product cards contain a result story and up to three layered screenshots.
- Detailed project screenshots rise and collect as a sticky card stack.
- Rebuilt “Bring the problem” as a playful incoming-message scene.
- Moved About to the final chapter and restored Khushi's portrait there.
